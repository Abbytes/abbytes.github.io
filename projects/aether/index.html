About this piece

Aether is an interactive particle field. Move (or drag) to swirl the motes; click or tap to burst a vortex. Hold for a stronger pull.

Tune particle count, force, and trails. Shift color — Ice, Ember, Moss, Prism, Silver — and the ground: Void, Ink, Graphite, Abyss, Parchment. Download a still of the field with no chrome.
